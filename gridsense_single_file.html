About The Initiative

Building the Intelligence Layer for National Infrastructure.

GridSense combines spatial graph neural networks (GNN), physics-informed power flow digital twins, and operator-first explainability to transform national grid resilience.

Our Mission

To anticipate and eliminate large-scale cascading blackouts before they begin through explainable causal intelligence.

Who We Serve

National grid operators, regional load dispatch centers (RLDC), and transmission utilities managing high-voltage networks.

Our Architecture

Spatial-temporal GraphSAGE neural network coupled with AC power flow simulations and Shapley feature attribution.
